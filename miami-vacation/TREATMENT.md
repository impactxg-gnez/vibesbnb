# Miami vacation — treatment

Style: Time-lapse Symphony (`city-symphony`). Topic: a relaxing, modern, classy Miami that can sit behind the VibesBNB headline. Picture only, no narrator. On-screen type stays off this cut: the site already sets the words (“Find your … sanctuary”), and burned-in titles would land in the same left column.

## Three structures

1. **One glide inland.** Water, then a single street, then night. Too thin: Miami reads as a postcard, not a city.
2. **Day streets, a held breath, night.** Coast and three street passages by day, one slowed fragile thing (water against a seawall), then downtown light after dark, and a short black breath on the bar. Chosen.
3. **Neighborhood list.** Sunny Isles, Brickell, South Beach as equal chapters with a name card each. The cards would fight the headline, and three labels make a travel reel.

Chosen: **2**. The opening image is sun on water and a line of palms, not a skyline logo. The ending image is the same kind of street after dark, so the site loop can return to day without a new idea. The score stays in one key and never climbs into a full-ensemble frenzy: this film has to be readable under large type.

## Logline and arc

Miami in one afternoon: the coast gives way to streets, the streets slow down at the water, and the city comes back as light.

Setup: a calm coast and modern streets in hard daylight. Turn: motion almost stops on the water. Ending: the same downtown, now only headlights and windows, then a black breath on the bar.

## Benchmark

- Learn from *Koyaanisqatsi*’s street time and from a quiet luxury-hotel film (slow aerials, warm highlights, no presenter): how a city can feel expensive when the camera does not hurry and the grade is one print.
- Do not take Glass’s notes, Reggio’s titles, any hotel logo, or the GTA framing of the source reel’s original upload title. The Commons page is the source; the film never names a game.

All from the same reel, hard cuts, 30 fps, 100 BPM (1 beat = 18 frames, 1 bar = 72 frames = 2.4 s). Light 35 mm grade on every shot. Slow push 1.00 → 1.06 inside each shot.

## Cue map

100 BPM, 4/4, one chord per bar, A minor 7 → F major 9 → C major 7 → G suspended, then the same four chords again under the brake and the night. Cuts on bar lines only. Instrumentation: soft organ pad and a sparse flute-range figure in quarters, never sixteenths. No drums. The black breath cuts the dry signal; the site loop has no black, so its file is silent and the page plays it muted.

## Sound

No source audio (the Commons file is silent, and the style does not use footage soundtracks). No ambience bed, no foley. Music only, on the film file. The hero on the site is muted so it can autoplay.

## Type

None burned in. No number cards, no quote card: both would sit on the headline. Subtitles: none. Credits live in `CREDITS`, not on the picture.
